Dynasties — Mechanics Build v1

Purpose:
- Plain grass-only battlefield
- Perfect gameplay mechanics before returning to detailed art

Rules:
- 1 hero per team at start
- 300 starting florins each
- +100 florins per full turn
- 1 barracks per team
- Basic Soldier costs 300
- Barracks can recruit once per turn
- New recruits act next turn
- Units have individual movement
- Units block movement
- Barracks block movement
- One attack per unit per turn
- Attacking ends remaining movement
- Harper AI recruits, moves and attacks
- Automatic local save
