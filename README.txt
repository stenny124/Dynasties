Dynasties — Mechanics Build v15

Changes from v14:
- Added Cavalry for both House Stark and House Lannister.
- Cavalry stats:
  * HP 8
  * ATK 4
  * DEF 2
  * MOV 4
  * RNG 1
  * Cost 600 gold
- Added cavalry recruitment button at Winterfell / Casterly Rock.
- Updated all Soldier sprites to the new helmeted Stark/Lannister versions.
- Updated all Archer sprites to the new helmeted Stark/Lannister versions.
- Added new Stark/Lannister Cavalry sprites.
- Archers are now ENGAGED by adjacent enemy Soldiers OR Cavalry and cannot move away that turn.
- AI can now choose to save for and recruit Cavalry, especially in response to archer-heavy armies.
- Enemy tactical priorities were updated so cavalry strongly hunts archers and acts before other units.
